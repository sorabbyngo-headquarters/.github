## Why We Exist

Africa generates enormous amounts of data — health records, financial transactions, logistics movements, communications, agricultural outputs. Today, that data flows through infrastructure owned and controlled by entities outside Africa.

That is not an accident. It is a structural problem with structural consequences: African data processed under foreign laws, African users subject to foreign policy decisions, African economies dependent on foreign technology stacks for basic digital operations.

We are fixing it. Not by advocating for policy change — by building the alternative.

## Our Principles

**Sovereignty first.** Technology that African individuals, companies, and governments depend on should be owned and controlled by African entities.

**Built for the continent.** We design for the CHW in rural Kisii with an intermittent data connection, not the developer in San Francisco with fibre.

**Open where it matters.** Sovereign infrastructure that nobody can audit is not truly sovereign.

**Excellence without apology.** We hold ourselves to a global standard.

**Long-term thinking.** Infrastructure takes decades to build and lasts for generations.
